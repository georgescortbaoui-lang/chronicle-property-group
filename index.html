BEFORE YOU DECIDE

A few good
questions.

Clear expectations make a better working relationship.

Do I need a membership to book assistance?+

No. The published assistance tariff is the same for everyone. Membership provides the recurring walkthrough schedule, reports and progressive property record; it isn’t a discount club.

Is there a large initial documentation fee?+

No separate standard property-record fee is charged at the start. Essential access, contacts and instructions are established first. We build the agreed additional record throughout your membership term, with its scope and completion date confirmed in your quote.

What if I only need care while I’m away?+

Tell us your departure and return dates. We’ll agree a service schedule and price, including how your Property Chronicle milestones fit that term. Pausing service never implies visits continue.

What if a task takes longer than expected?+

We agree the estimated time and your authorized maximum before starting. Additional ordinary time is $20 per 15 minutes. We seek approval before exceeding the agreed allowance; work is not retrospectively moved to a higher category.

Does homewatch replace a technical inspection or insurance?+

No. Walkthroughs are visual and non-invasive, within an agreed accessible scope. They don’t certify hidden systems, guarantee against damage or replace your insurer’s requirements. Repairs and technical assessments belong with appropriately qualified providers.

Can you attend during or immediately after a storm?+

Storm work is separately agreed against available crew capacity. Evacuation orders, unsafe conditions, closed roads and community restrictions take priority. We do not promise attendance during unsafe conditions or a fixed post-storm arrival time.
